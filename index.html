Privacy Policy — MP Exam Sikhar

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

MP Exam Sikhar ("we", "our", or "the app") is an educational application designed to help students prepare for competitive examinations through study materials, quizzes, practice tests, scores, rankings, and related learning features.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, processed, and deleted when you use MP Exam Sikhar. By using the app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process the following information:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and account identifier when you sign in using Google Login.
  • Learning information: Quiz answers, quiz scores, practice progress, match results, rankings, and leaderboard information where these features are used.
  • App and technical information: Technical information required to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the app.
  • Advertising information: Information that may be collected by Google AdMob, such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, ad interactions, and other information described in Google's applicable policies.

3. Google Login

MP Exam Sikhar supports Google Login. When you sign in, Google may provide account information such as your name, email address, and account identifier, depending on the sign-in configuration and permissions.

We use this information to authenticate users and provide account features, save learning progress, and display relevant scores or rankings.

4. Firebase and Cloud Firestore

The app uses Firebase services, including Cloud Firestore, to support account-related information and app features. Depending on the features used, this may include user profiles, quiz scores, practice progress, match results, and leaderboard information.

Information is processed and stored according to the app's implementation and applicable Firebase configuration. Google's services may process information under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Google AdMob and Advertising

MP Exam Sikhar uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about ad delivery or interactions, where applicable.

Advertising practices, personalized advertising, and consent requirements depend on applicable laws, the user's location, and the app's advertising configuration.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Google Login and account functionality.
  • Save and display quiz scores and learning progress.
  • Operate leaderboards, rankings, and multiplayer features.
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Identify and fix technical problems.
  • Improve app functionality and user experience.
  • Respond to privacy requests and comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google Login, Firebase, and Google AdMob, as necessary to provide app functionality, authenticate users, store app data, display advertisements, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

Scores, rankings, or display names may be visible to other users when required by leaderboard or multiplayer features. Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in public-facing profile fields.

8. Data Storage and Security

Account-related and learning information may be stored using Firebase services. We use the security measures implemented by our app and service providers to help protect information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security in every situation.

9. Account and Data Deletion

MP Exam Sikhar provides an account deletion option within the app. Users can use this option to request deletion of their account and associated data.

When the user confirms account deletion, the app's deletion process deletes the user's account and associated data covered by that process.

This may include the user's account information, profile data, quiz scores, learning progress, match results, and leaderboard records stored by the app, where those records are included in the deletion process.

Information may be retained where required by law or where limited retention is necessary for legitimate security purposes. Data independently retained by third-party providers, including advertising service providers, may be subject to their own retention and deletion policies.

If you experience a problem with account deletion, contact us using the support email provided below.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.
