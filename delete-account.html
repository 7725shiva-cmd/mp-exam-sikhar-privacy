Delete Your MP Exam Sikhar Account

Users of MP Exam Sikhar can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

Email your account deletion request to the app developer using the contact email listed on the Google Play Store.

Include your Google account email and the subject: Account Deletion Request - MP Exam Sikhar.

Data deletion

We will review your request and delete the associated account data that we control, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Contact

For account deletion requests, contact the developer using the contact details listed on the Google Play Store.
